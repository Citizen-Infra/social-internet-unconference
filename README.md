# Social Internet Unconference

An online unconference on funding the social internet, co-organized with [Roomy](https://roomy.space).

This repo is the **planning home** while the event is still being shaped. It is not the event site. Nothing here is announced — most of it is still undecided, and the open questions below are the actual work.

## Status

**Nothing is settled yet.** As of 2026-07-21 there is agreement to do it, one locked theme, and a proposed venue. No name, no date, no format, no invite list.

## What this is

An online unconference in the classic format — participants register, agree the topics together at the start, then move between rooms. Not a keynote conference with a fixed agenda.

The framing is deliberately cross-community: not a CIBC event and not a Roomy event, but a shared one that pulls in several adjacent communities around a common set of questions.

## Who does what

- **Artem Zhiganov (CIBC)** — drives and coordinates.
- **Erlend Sogge Heggen (Roomy)** — contributes shaping and theming ideas, and attends. He was explicit that coordination is not his role.

Nobody else is committed yet.

## Themes

One theme is locked:

- **Technologists who want a different kind of funding** — the non-VC, reinvestment-oriented end of software funding.

Erlend described this as roughly one of three or four central themes. **The others are undecided** and are not to be invented — see issue #3.

## Proposed infrastructure

Both proposed, neither confirmed.

- **Venue: [Streamplace](https://stream.place)** — a Twitch-like streaming platform running on AT Proto, built on top of Livepeer's decentralized streaming infrastructure. Its existing use is AT Proto developers livestreaming their own development sessions and teams streaming shared meetings, which is adjacent to running unconference rooms. Nobody from Streamplace has been approached yet — see issue #2.
- **Coordination space: Roomy** — Erlend is setting up a single shared space with a few channels covering both this unconference and the wider open-source funding discussion. One combined space rather than two, on the reasoning that it is better not to start out more fragmented than necessary.

## Open questions

Each of these is an issue in this repo.

2. Is Streamplace actually available and willing?

4. Who joins the Roomy coordination space, and what channels does it need?

## Background

This came out of a conversation between Artem and Erlend on 2026-07-21 about AT Proto, open-source funding models, and how the independent-developer ecosystem sustains itself. The unconference is the concrete thing that came out of it.
