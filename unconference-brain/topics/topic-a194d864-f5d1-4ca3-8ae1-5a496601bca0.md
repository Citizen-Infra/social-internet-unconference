Across both sessions, a recurring concern is how builders and indie developers are financially sustained within open protocols. This spans the ATProto upstream approval-lag risk, the inadequacy of AI profit-sharing as a remedy for extractive harm, NLnet's focus on ActivityPub over ATProto, and the unresolved question of whether alternative investors and open-web builders can be connected at scale.
