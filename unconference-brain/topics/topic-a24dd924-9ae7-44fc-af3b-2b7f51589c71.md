The Erlend-Artem session surfaces a normative disagreement about what success should look like for indie founders — philanthropy versus becoming investors in other indies — alongside a critique that AI profit-sharing is insufficient if the extractive model continues. Both point toward a preference for reciprocal, reinvestment-oriented structures over one-directional giving.
