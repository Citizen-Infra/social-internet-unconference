Across both sessions, a consistent concern emerges that existing open-source and ATProto funding mechanisms are structurally inaccessible to independent builders outside major hubs — not because the money doesn't exist but because discovery, qualification, and navigation are prohibitively difficult. This surfaces as a proposed SIU session topic, an open question about the single biggest friction point, and a bilateral conversation about geographic exclusion and the chicken-and-egg problem of connecting investors to builders.
