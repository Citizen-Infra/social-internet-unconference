Multiple proposals and tensions circle the same structural problem: federated communities need coordination (on identity portability, moderation escalation, feed governance, and memory retention) but any cross-community mechanism risks overriding local authority. This theme runs through the portable identity, restorative moderation, community-owned feeds, and durable memory proposals.
